Hi, I'm Hakkı Kaan.

I'm 20 and study Computer Programming at Akdeniz University in Antalya. This page is my route map: where I came from, the stops I passed and where I'm heading.

1Me, briefly

I can't leave something alone until I know how it works. While I learn HTML, CSS, Python and databases at school, I explore what AI agents can do.

I graduated from Akdeniz Anatolian High School in 2024. I kept making the content I started in high school, played the guitar I taught myself on the street with friends, then worked as a cook for 8 months. After that, I decided to study.

My favourite thing is teaching AI my own method. I do it by writing SKILL.md files; one of them, self-improving-skills, is published on GitHub under the MIT licence.

“AI doesn't think for me; I use it to try things faster.”

— Hakkı Kaan Yeşil
