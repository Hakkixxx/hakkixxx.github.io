My Photoshop work from the Motion Graphics 1 course. Each project is a stop on the orange line: see the before, the after and how I made it.

  1. 1

    Project 1 · Photoshop · 2026

    Selective colour with the History Brush

    I turned the whole image black and white, then painted only the foreground character back to colour with the History Brush. The background stays grey, so the eye goes straight to the character.

    1. 1Converted the image to black and white
    2. 2Set the colour state as the source in History
    3. 3Painted the foreground with the History Brush
  2. 2

    Project 2 · Photoshop · 2026

    Product ad: iPhone 17

    I made an ad visual for a phone. I placed the product on a soft, light background and added a reflection and shadow under it so it looks like it stands on a glossy surface. Three short slogans sit next to it.

    1. 1Cut the product out of its background
    2. 2Reflection from a flipped copy and a mask
    3. 3Soft shadow and slogan typography
  3. 6

    Project 6 · Photoshop · 960 Grid System · 2026

    Web interface: Demre Municipality

    I took the real Demre Municipality website apart and rebuilt it in Photoshop on the 12-column 960 Grid System. With Layer Comps I made three different page layouts in the same file; switch between them with the tabs.

    1. 1Set up the 12-column 960 grid
    2. 2Made each section its own layer group
    3. 3Saved 3 layouts with Layer Comps

    Source: the live Demre Municipality website: demre.bel.tr

    demre-belediyesi.psd · Layout 1

    Scroll inside the frame.

  4. 3

    Project 3 · Photoshop · 2026

    Colouring a pencil drawing

    I coloured a black-line 'Haydi Dansa' drawing. The lines sit on the top layer in Multiply mode and the colours are painted region by region on separate layers underneath, so I can change any colour without touching the line art.

    1. 1Set the line layer to Multiply
    2. 2Painted skin, clothes and wall on separate layers
    3. 3Added depth with shading

    Source drawing: karakalem.info (Can Vanci)

  5. 4

    Project 4 · Photoshop · Smart Object · 2026

    Swapping billboards with Smart Objects

    I turned the four billboards on a night highway into Smart Objects. Editing the poster inside once updated all four at once: the 'Summer Festival' ad became a 'Winter Festival' ad. The far billboard stays out of focus with a Gaussian Blur smart filter.

    1. 1Made the billboards Smart Objects in a 'panolar' group
    2. 2Edited the poster inside and saved
    3. 3Added a Gaussian Blur smart filter to the far billboard
